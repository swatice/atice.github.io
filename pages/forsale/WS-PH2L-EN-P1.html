About this book:

FUNDAMENTALS OF PHYSICS, Part Two, contains the second semester material of a four-semester physics course for students of physics and other natural and engineering sciences that require a solid basic knowledge of physics. This second volume offers students an in-depth introduction to electromagnetism. Following a brief introduction to the subject and the mathematical tools required for electromagnetism, the subsequent twelve chapters cover: the fundamentals of electrostatics and electrostatic fields; the behavior of bodies in electric fields and of electric fields within bodies; the properties of dielectric matter; the fundamentals of magnetostatics and magnetic fields; the magnetic moment and electromagnetic induction; the properties of magnetic matter; direct and alternating current; time-dependent electric and magnetic fields; and electromagnetic waves.

For whom could this book be of interest?

This book was written for college students majoring in natural sciences or engineering fields as an introduction into the fundamentals of physics. However, younger readers such as high school honor students with an interest in physics & engineering should also be able to work through the material with significant gain. Of course, this introductory text should also be of interest to science readers in general.
